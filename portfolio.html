Case-study framework

Proposed design exercise. None of the stages below have been completed yet. This is the structure I will follow when I redesign one of the projects above, and I’ll replace this section with real findings once they exist.

  1. Problem

    The specific difficulty a person has with the current screen.

  2. Intended users

    Who this is for and in what situation.

  3. Assumptions to validate

    What I believe but haven’t yet checked.

  4. User flow

    The path from first tap to finished task.

  5. Wireframes

    Low-fidelity layouts to compare structure.

  6. Visual design

    Type, color, spacing and states.

  7. Prototype

    A clickable version to test with.

  8. Feedback

    Notes from a few real people trying it.

  9. Iterations

    What changed and why.

  10. What I learned

    What I would do differently next time.

Design process

This is how I aim to work. It isn’t a claim that I’ve completed every step on every project.

  1. 1

    Understand

    Ask who it’s for and what they’re trying to do. Write down assumptions so they can be checked.

  2. 2

    Structure

    Map the user flow and sketch wireframes before choosing colors, so the layout carries the logic.

  3. 3

    Explore

    Try more than one direction, then build a prototype in Figma or in code.

  4. 4

    Validate

    Run a lightweight usability test, watch where people hesitate, fix it, and explain the decision to the team.
